B-AMT: Start of an Amount (e.g., "1").
I-AMT: Continuation of an Amount (e.g., "/2" in "1/2").
B-UNIT: Start of a Unit (e.g., "cup").
I-UNIT: Continuation of a Unit (e.g., "ounce" in "fluid ounce").
B-NAME: Start of the Ingredient Name (e.g., "olive").
I-NAME: Continuation of the Ingredient Name (e.g., "oil").
B-DESC: Start of a Descriptor/Adjective (e.g., "chopped").
I-DESC: Continuation of a Descriptor (e.g., "chopped" in "finely chopped").
O: Outside (Punctuation, "and", "or", irrelevant words).
